WHAT THE NUMBERS MEAN

A trade, measured properly.

A win means dealing more of the enemy’s HP than you lose in one expected exchange. Accuracy, additive camouflage, dodge, critical hits, anti-crit, damage reduction, ammo and Rapid Fire are included. A win rate is the share of valid opposing loadouts beaten, excluding self; it is not a duel win probability.

Weighted metrics give stronger opponents more influence, using their unweighted win rates. The best counter to one enemy need not be the best setup overall.

Data & reproducibility

Loadouts and all matrix entries are stored in lossless, double-precision Parquet. Only the selected enemy’s matchup slice is fetched. Inventory images are processed on your device; screenshots are never uploaded. Recognition identifies item artwork, not its numerical tier or rolled stats. Imported gear must match the fixed model above.
